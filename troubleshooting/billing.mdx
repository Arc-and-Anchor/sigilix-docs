<Note>
Sigilix is in **private beta**. There are no public self-serve plans yet — no Free/Pro/Max tiers, no checkout, no published prices. Billing is handled per-account during the beta.
</Note>

## How billing works during the beta

While Sigilix is in private beta, your account's terms — what's included, how usage is counted, and any cost — are arranged directly with us when you're onboarded. Nothing is charged automatically, and there's no plan to pick.

If you want to start using Sigilix, the front door is **"Join our private beta"** on the marketing site.

## What pricing will look like

When Sigilix opens up, pricing is expected to combine, conceptually:

- **A per-seat component** — for the people on your team using reviews, the CLI, and Deep-Research Chat.
- **A usage component** — scaled to how much review work you run.
- **Bring-your-own models (BYO)** — point Sigilix at your own model access (Codex CLI, Claude Code, or your own SDK/keys) so model spend stays on your side.

We're not publishing specific numbers here while the model is still being finalized. The marketing pricing page is the canonical place those land.

## Questions about billing

For anything billing-related during the beta — onboarding terms, usage questions, invoices — contact [support@sigilix.ai](mailto:support@sigilix.ai).
