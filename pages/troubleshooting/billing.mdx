# Billing

Sigilix plans combine seats with usage allowances. The portal is the live source for your account's current plan, seat count, and usage state; the marketing pricing page is the source for current prices and packaging.

<Callout type="info">
This page explains billing concepts. For the exact pool and reset mechanics, use [Usage & Limits](/usage-limits).
</Callout>

## Plan shape

The current product tiers are Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, and Enterprise. Usage differs by tier: Free is capped, Pro is the standard paid window, Max has 5x Pro's usage windows, Ultra has 10x Max's usage windows, Team has more usage than Pro for teams, and Enterprise is scaled to the organization and contract.

Exact request counts are not published in the docs or the portal usage-limit copy. The portal Usage panel is the authoritative source for the allowance currently applied to your account.

## What billing covers

- **Seats** for people using Sigilix CLI, chat, and related per-seat workflows.
- **Per-seat usage** for coding-agent and chatbot activity.
- **Organization usage** for PR reviews, Linear triage, and Slack usage.
- **Bring-your-own provider routing** where enabled for CLI and chat model workflows.
