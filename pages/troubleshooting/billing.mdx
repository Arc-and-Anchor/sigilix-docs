# Billing

<Callout type="info">
Sigilix is **live and self-serve**. Start on the Free plan, or [start a 14-day Pro trial](https://app.sigilix.ai/login?intent=trial&plan=pro) — no card required to try. See the [pricing page](https://sigilix.ai/pricing) for current numbers.
</Callout>

## Plans

Sigilix has five self-serve plans plus Enterprise. Pricing and included usage are published on the [pricing page](https://sigilix.ai/pricing) — the canonical source:

- **Free** — PR review with a low-cap usage window. No card required.
- **Pro** — triage, review, CLI, and Deep-Research Chat, with a **14-day trial**.
- **Teams** — all features across a seat range, billed per seat.
- **Max** — more usage and deeper reasoning.
- **Ultra** — the largest usage windows and org controls.
- **Enterprise** — for scale and compliance; [talk to us](mailto:sales@sigilix.ai).

## How you're billed

Pricing combines:

- **A per-seat component** — for the people on your team using reviews, the CLI, and Deep-Research Chat.
- **A usage component** — scaled to how much review work you run. Usage windows reset on a 5-day cadence — see [Rate Limits](/configuration/rate-limits).
- **Bring-your-own-key (BYOK)** — on paid tiers, connect your own model-provider key (Anthropic, OpenAI, or another provider) so model spend stays on your side. See [Bring Your Own Key](/cli-and-chat/bring-your-own-models).

Manage your plan, seats, and payment method from the [Sigilix dashboard](https://app.sigilix.ai).

## Questions about billing

For anything billing-related — plan changes, usage questions, invoices — contact [support@sigilix.ai](mailto:support@sigilix.ai).
